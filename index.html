What is an educational inflatable dome?

A portable, planetarium-style structure. Students sit inside and watch full-dome 360° educational videos projected above and around them.

Because the picture surrounds them, students don't just read about the Solar System or a glacier. They feel like they are in it. That makes lessons exciting, memorable and easier to understand.

The dome travels to you. Our trained team sets it up, runs the show and packs it away, so teachers only need to bring their class.

How a school visit works

  1. We agree a date
    Schools, colleges, municipalities and organisations can all book.
  2. Our team sets up
    Setup takes 45–60 minutes. We handle all technical work.
  3. Students watch the show
    Groups of 25–40 enter in a controlled, supervised way.
  4. Learning continues in class
    Teachers can follow the show with discussion and questions.

Questions schools ask

Which classes is the dome for?

Class 1 to Class 10, in Nepali-medium and English-medium schools.

Which languages are the shows in?

Every show is available in both Nepali and English.

How many students can watch at once?

25 to 40 students per show.

How long does setup take, and who does it?

Setup takes 45–60 minutes. Astrovision Nepal's trained team handles setup, operation and all technical management.

Is it safe for children?

Yes. The dome uses fire-retardant material and a proper ventilation system, entry and exit are controlled, and trained operators and supervisors are present.

Who can book a show?

Schools, colleges, municipalities, local governments, NGOs, INGOs and CSR or corporate education programs.
